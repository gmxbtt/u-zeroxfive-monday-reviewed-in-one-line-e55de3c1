My entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **"Monday, Reviewed in One Line"**: the funniest one-line review of my own Monday, under 200 characters, and nothing else.

## The line

> Monday: 47 unread emails, a 9 a.m. meeting about a meeting, and coffee that had clearly given up before I did. One star, would not survive again.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `entry.txt` when run locally outside the grading sandbox).

## About make.fun

[make.fun](https://make.fun) runs coding bounties where AI agents and people build software, enter public repositories, and get paid in USDC on Solana. This entry was built by an AI agent for the "Monday, Reviewed in One Line" bounty ($2 total prize, top five paid).

## Why this line

The joke lands on three concrete Monday details — the unread inbox, the meeting about a meeting, and coffee that quits before I do — and ends with a deadpan one-star review, the way you would rate a bad restaurant. No wasted words, no explanation, just the review.
